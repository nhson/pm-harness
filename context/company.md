# Tallyo (synthetic company, fictional data)

Tallyo is a B2B invoicing and accounts-receivable SaaS for small and mid-sized service businesses (agencies, consultancies, IT services) in the EU. Founded 2021, ~2,400 paying accounts, €3.1M ARR.

Plans: Starter €19/mo (1 user), Team €59/mo (5 users), Business €149/mo (unlimited users, API, multi-entity).
Core product: create and send invoices, recurring invoices, payment links (card + SEPA), reminders, basic AR dashboard, accounting export (CSV, DATEV).
Current metrics: trial-to-paid 14%, monthly logo churn 2.6% (Starter 4.1%, Team 2.2%, Business 0.9%), median days-sales-outstanding (DSO) for customers 38 days.
